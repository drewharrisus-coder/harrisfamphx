Food safety, quickly

Thaw in the fridge only, never on the counter. If it's still icy Wednesday, put it breast-down in a sink of cold water, about 30 min per pound, and change the water every 30 min.

165°F in the thickest part of the thigh and the center of the breast. The dressing has raw egg and giblets, so it should reach 165°F in the center too.

Leftovers go in the fridge within 2 hours. Eat within 3–4 days, or freeze.
